Diabetes Risk Estimator

A Random Forest trained on the CDC’s BRFSS 2015 survey. Fill in the profile; the estimate updates as you go.

–

Educational project, not medical advice. The data is from the US in 2015, and the model favors recall () over precision (), so it flags many people. The percentage is the share of people with diabetes or pre-diabetes among test-set profiles with a similar model score. A blood glucose test is the only way to know.
